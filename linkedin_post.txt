# LinkedIn post — Telecom Customer Churn Prediction
# (publish after GitHub push; replace REPO_URL)

From predictions to prevention 📊

New project: predicting telecom customer churn with machine learning.

Using 7,043 real customer records (IBM's telco dataset), I built and
benchmarked three models — logistic regression, random forest, and gradient
boosting — with proper handling of class imbalance and 5-fold
cross-validation.

The winner surprised me: plain logistic regression (ROC-AUC 0.841, 78%
recall) beat both tree ensembles. Sometimes the simpler model wins, and
that's worth reporting honestly.

The business payoff: the model flags a high-risk segment of 1,783 customers
churning at 62.6% vs 26.5% overall — month-to-month contracts, fiber optic,
no tech support. That's exactly who a retention campaign should target in a
customer's first 6 months.

Top churn drivers: tenure (by far), internet service, contract type,
monthly charges.
